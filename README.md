## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
The platform will work in the following way:
1. Farmer Registration: Farmers can create an account and upload their products, quantity, expected harvest date and selling price.
2. Direct Buying: Consumers and bulk buyers can view available products, compare prices and place orders directly with farmers.
3. Harvest Pre-Booking: Consumers can book vegetables and fruits before the harvest. This helps farmers understand demand before bringing their products to market.
4. HarvestShare – Group Ordering: People living in the same apartment, street or nearby area can combine their orders. Farmers can supply larger quantities in a single delivery.
5. Know Your Farmer: Every product can have a QR code showing the farmer's name, farm location, harvest date and product details.
6. Surplus Selling: If a farmer has unsold products, the platform can notify nearby buyers and offer those products for sale before they go to waste.
How it addresses the problem
- Reduces unnecessary intermediaries between farmers and buyers.
- Allows farmers to receive a better share of the selling price.
- Helps consumers purchase fresh products at transparent prices.
- Reduces transportation costs through combined orders.
- Helps farmers avoid producing or harvesting without understanding market demand.
Innovation and uniqueness

The main innovation is not simply connecting farmers and consumers. It combines three ideas:
- Harvest Pre-Booking: Know the demand before harvest.
- HarvestShare: Combine nearby orders to reduce delivery costs.
- Know Your Farmer: Build trust through product traceability.
This makes Farm2Home more than an ordinary online grocery platform.

## Feasibility and Viability
Analysis of feasibility

The project is technically feasible because it can be developed using commonly available technologies such as Python, Django, MySQL and mapping services.

We can start with a small group of farmers and consumers in one locality before expanding to other areas.

Potential challenges and risks
1. Farmers may have limited digital knowledge.
2. Some farmers may not have access to smartphones or stable internet.
3. Transportation and delivery arrangements may be difficult.
4. Product quality and quantity may differ from the listed details.
5. Farmers may not receive enough orders for certain products.
Strategies to overcome challenges
- Provide a simple interface in Tamil and other regional languages.
- Allow FPOs or farmer representatives to manage product listings.
- Combine orders from nearby customers to reduce delivery expenses.
- Display harvest dates and product information to improve transparency.
- Use pre-booking and demand prediction to reduce unsold stock.
- Start with local delivery partners and gradually expand.
